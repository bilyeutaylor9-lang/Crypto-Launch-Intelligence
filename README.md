# Crypto Launch Intelligence

## Overview

Crypto Launch Intelligence is an AI-powered crypto market intelligence platform designed to discover promising blockchain projects at their earliest stages.

Rather than chasing projects that are already trending, the platform identifies measurable improvements in project quality, ecosystem growth, developer activity, on-chain momentum, liquidity, and community acceleration.

The objective is to surface projects that are strengthening before they become widely recognized.

# Vision

Build the industry's most comprehensive early-stage crypto intelligence platform.

The platform combines:
